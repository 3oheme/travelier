description: |
  Rio Acima, a small town in the hills of Minas Gerais, Brazil. Summer night in the countryside. It begins with light rain on the vegetation, on the roof, on the earth. Then the thunder comes — not the rolling coastal kind, but something bigger and closer, the kind that comes down from the hills and reverberates across the valley.

  This is different from tropical coastal rain. The countryside absorbs it differently. The night insects continue. A dog barks once, far away. The rain intensifies, and then pulls back, and then intensifies again.

  Thirty minutes of countryside Brazilian night. Recorded with Schoeps CCM4 microphones in ORTF configuration by felix.blume.

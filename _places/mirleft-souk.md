description: |
  Mirleft, a small coastal town in the south of Morocco, on market day. The weekly souk fills the main street with colour and noise. A vendor calls through a megaphone, the amplified voice rising and falling above the crowd. Mopeds weave between stalls and people, their engines buzzing and fading. Berber is spoken all around — quick, warm, unhurried in the negotiating.

  The afternoon wears on. The light flattens. And then, as evening comes, the call to prayer lifts from the mosque and spreads across the market, over the stalls and the voices and the mopeds, settling everything into a quieter register for a moment. Then the souk resumes.

  Recorded walking through the market with OKM binaural microphones on a Roland R09HR. The sense of place is immediate — you are in the crowd.
